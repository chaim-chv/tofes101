# Studies credit

## 1. Academic degree (s. 40ג)

| Degree | Points | Maximum years | Start |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bachelor’s (תואר ראשון) | **1.0** | 3 tax years | the tax year **after** graduation |
| Master’s (תואר שני) | **0.5** | 2 tax years | the tax year after graduation |
| Medicine / dentistry (third degree) | 1.0 for 3 years + 0.5 for 2 years | as stated | after graduation |
| Direct PhD track | 1.0 for the bachelor component + 0.5 for the doctorate | as stated | after the relevant graduation |

- The credit is given for **one** bachelor’s and **one** master’s only (s. 40ג(ה)).
- It requires a recognised institution and a certificate of completion/eligibility.
- For a profession requiring internship (התמחות), the graduate may choose the year
  after graduation or the year after completing the internship, provided the
  internship starts no later than the year after graduation (s. 40ג(ג1)).
- The number of years is the number of academic years, capped at 3 (BA) / 2 (MA).

## 2. Profession studies (s. 40ד)

- **1 point** for a recognised professional certificate (תעודת מקצוע).
- Up to **3 tax years**, starting the year after completion.
- “Professional studies” = at least 1,700 study hours, recognised by a government
  ministry.
- No double claim with the academic track: choose one (s. 40ה).

## 3. Teaching certificate

- A recognised teaching certificate (תעודת הוראה) can give **1 point**, for up to
  3 years, under the applicable regulations.
- If the person also has an academic/profession credit, choose the better one; do
  not stack (s. 40ה).
- The exact statutory instrument is a regulation, not s. 40ג; mark `likely` if the
  user’s certificate is not a standard Ministry of Education one.

## 4. Form 101 item 15 and form 119

- Item 15: “בגין סיום לימודים לתואר אקדמי, סיום התמחות או סיום לימודי מקצוע”.
- Document: a declaration on **form 119** (application for the academic/profession
  credit). Form 119 is **not** tax coordination.
- The employer may apply the credit monthly once the form 119/approval is provided;
  otherwise the employee claims it in the annual return.
- The 2026 electronic matrix requires the form 119 declaration as the attachment.

## 5. Transitional rules

- For graduates in 2014–2022, the old rule gave the credit for **one year** (with a
  choice of year). The current multi-year rule applies to later graduates.
- If the user graduated in the transition period, ask for the graduation year and
  apply the rule for that year; do not assume the current multi-year rule.

## 6. Implementation

1. Collect: degree type, institution, graduation year, internship (if any), and
   whether a form 119 approval exists.
2. Compute the start year and the number of years; cap at 3/2.
3. Do not allow both academic and profession credits for the same studies.
4. Do not apply the credit before the year after graduation (or the approved
   internship year).
5. Require form 119 / the approval before allowing the claim.
6. Year-stamp the credit; it can span multiple tax years.

## 7. Unverified
