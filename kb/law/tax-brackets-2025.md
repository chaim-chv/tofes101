# 2025 brackets

## 1. Annual (personal effort / work income)

| Rate | Taxable annual income | Tax on the band |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10% | up to 84,120 ₪ | up to 8,412 ₪ |
| 14% | 84,121–120,720 ₪ | up to 5,124 ₪ |
| 20% | 120,721–193,800 ₪ | up to 14,616 ₪ |
| 31% | 193,801–269,280 ₪ | up to 23,399 ₪ |
| 35% | 269,281–560,280 ₪ | up to 101,850 ₪ |
| 47% | above 560,280 ₪ | — |

## 2. Monthly equivalent

| Rate | Monthly income |
| --- | --- |
| 10% | up to 7,010 ₪ |
| 14% | 7,011–10,060 ₪ |
| 20% | 10,061–16,150 ₪ |
| 31% | 16,151–22,440 ₪ |
| 35% | 22,441–46,690 ₪ |
| 47% | above 46,690 ₪ |

## 3. Surtax

- **3%** on taxable income above **721,560 ₪/year** (60,130 ₪/month), s. 121ב.
- **+2%** on capital-source income above the same threshold, from 2025 (freeze
  law).
- The employer withholds the 3% on salary above the ceiling; the 2% capital
  surtax is normally outside payroll.

## 4. How credits enter

```
annual_tax = sum over bands
tax_after_credits = max(0, annual_tax - points × 2,904)
monthly_withholding = tax_after_credits / 12
```

2025 point value: 2,904 ₪/year (242 ₪/month). The 2.25 resident base is worth
6,534 ₪/year.

## 5. Notes

- These are the **pre-2026-widening** thresholds. Do not use them for 2026.
- “Income from personal effort” includes work, business and a pension that is
  taxed as work income; capital income has a separate schedule (not fully shown
  here).
- The 2025 table was already under the 2025–2027 freeze, so it equals the 2024
  table except where legislation changed.
