# Youth credit

## 1. The rule

An individual who **turned 16 but not 18** in the tax year gets **1 point**
(s. 40ב).

- It is in addition to the 2.25 resident base.
- A female youth also gets the 0.5 woman point, so a 16–17-year-old female resident
  can have 2.25 + 1 + 0.5 = **3.75 points**.
- An adult whose **spouse** is 16–17 also gets the point (s. 40ב: “או לבן־זוגו”).
- Form 101 section ח item 13: “מלאו לי או לבן/בת זוגי 16 שנים וטרם מלאו 18 שנים
  בשנת המס”.
- The employer verifies the birth year (the electronic matrix says to check the
  employee’s/spouse’s birth year).

## 2. Age boundary

| Age at end of tax year | Point? |
| --- | --- |
| 15 or younger | No |
| 16 | Yes (1) |
| 17 | Yes (1) |
| 18 | No (the youth point ends; the mother’s 0.5 child point may apply if the person is a parent, not to the youth) |

“Turned 16 but not 18” means the tax year in which the 16th birthday occurs and
the following year, up to the year before the 18th birthday. The point is annual;
no proration by birthday month (statutory wording; `likely` for the birth-year
proration).

## 3. Employment of minors

- The 101 is within the minor’s scope; the youth point is claimed on it.
- No parent/guardian signature requirement was found in the form/regulations.
- Employment of children under 15 is restricted by the Youth Labour Law; 15–18
  have hour/rest restrictions. Those are separate from the tax form.
- A minor’s employer must still withhold tax and may need a 101 to apply the
  point; without it, maximum withholding.

## 4. Interaction with other tracks

- A 16–17-year-old can also be a discharged soldier (unlikely) or a student; the
  credits stack unless a specific rule says otherwise.
- The youth point is not the same as the child point the parent gets for the
  minor; the parent’s child point and the minor’s own youth point are separate
  taxpayers.

## 5. Implementation

1. Compute age at the end of the tax year from the birth date.
2. Enable item 13 only for age 16–17 (or spouse 16–17).
3. Do not ask for a document; the birth date is enough.
4. Add the 0.5 woman point if the youth is female.
5. Do not apply the youth point to a 15-year-old or an 18-year-old.
