# Disabled child credit

## 1. The rule

A parent of a **child without capacity** (ילד נטול יכולת) gets **2 points per
child per year** under s. 45.

- Value: **5,808 ₪/year** at 2,904 ₪/point (2025–2026).
- The child can be a minor or an adult child, if the statutory conditions are met.
- The credit is in addition to the age-based child points (the child may also be
  listed in section ג if under 19).
- Only **one parent** claims it, unless the parents are separated and do not share
  a household and the child receives the NII disability-child allowance — in that
  case each parent may claim it (subject to the conditions).
- The other parent must not claim the same points; the child must have no income
  in the current year (form item 11 conditions).

## 2. Eligibility routes

The s. 45 conditions include one of:

- the child receives **גמלת ילד נכה** from NII, or
- the child is **blind**, or
- the child is **100% disabled** / has a severe impairment as defined, or
- the child is in a defined “נטול יכולת” category (e.g. severe intellectual/
  developmental disability, bedridden, etc.).

The NII leaflet confirms the 2-point credit for a child up to 18 receiving the
disability-child allowance. The Tax Authority may require form 127 (medical
certificate) and form 116א for the institution-care alternative.

## 3. Form 101 item 11

- Label: “אני הורה לילדים עם מוגבלות שטרם מלאו להם 19 שנים, בגינם אני מקבל/ת
  גמלת ילד נכה מהמוסד לביטוח לאומי”.
- Tick + **count** of children.
- Attach **current-year NII disability-child approval**.
- The form’s conditions: child under 19; NII approval; spouse not claiming the
  same points; children have no income in the current year.
- For an adult child (18+), the 101 item 11 may not fit (it says under 19); the
  credit may still be claimed in the annual return under s. 45. **Likely.**

## 4. Alternative: institution care

- If the child is in an institution, the parent may choose between the 2 points
  and the **institution-care credit** under s. 45/44 (35% of qualifying expenses
  above 12.5% of income, capped). Do not claim both.
- The institution-care route is outside the 101’s item 11; route to form 116א /
  the annual return.

## 5. Interaction with other tracks

- The 2 points are **per child**, unlike the single-parent +1 (once).
- They stack with the age-based child points and with the single-parent +1.
- They are not the same as the employee’s own disability exemption (s. 9(5)),
  which is an income exemption, not a point.
- A child with a disability may also be a “child” for the age-based points, even
  after 18 for s. 45 purposes; the 101 child table stops at 19, so adult-child
  claims go to the annual return.

## 6. Implementation

1. Enable item 11 only if there is at least one child row (or an explicit “adult
   child” flag outside the 101).
2. Require the NII approval and the count.
3. Do not require the child’s ID to be an Israeli ID if the child is foreign/
   undocumented; route to the assessor.
4. Do not let both parents claim in the same household; the form asks the employee
   to confirm the spouse is not claiming.
5. Do not convert the 2 points into an exemption; it is a credit.
6. Year-stamp the approval (current year).
