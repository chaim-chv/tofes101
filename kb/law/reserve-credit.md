# Reserve combatant credit

## 1. The temporary 2026–2027 regime

For tax years **2026 and 2027**, an individual who served as a **combat reservist**
(לוחם) in the **previous tax year** gets points based on the number of reserve days:

| Days in the previous year | Points |
| --- | --- |
| 30–39 | **0.5** |
| 40–49 | **0.75** |
| 50+ | **1.0** |
| each additional 5 days beyond 50 | **+0.25** |
| **cap** | **4.0 points** |

- The credit is for the **year after** the service year: 2026 credit is based on
  2025 reserve days; 2027 credit on 2026 days.
- “לוחם” is defined by IDF orders; “reserve service” by the Reserve Service Law.
- Requires an IDF approval of combat-reserve entitlement.
- Form 101 section ח item 16: tick + enter the total number of reserve days in the
  previous tax year.

## 2. The permanent rule (outside the temporary window)

The permanent s. 39ב rule (for years before 2026 / after 2027) is:

- **0.75 point** for 20+ reserve days as a combatant;
- **+0.25 point per 5 additional days**;
- cap **4 points**.

The 2026–2027 temporary regime replaces the 20-day threshold with the 30/40/50
tiers. Do not apply the temporary tiers to 2025.

## 3. Interaction with other tracks

- The reserve credit is **in addition** to the 2.25 resident base and other credits.
- It can stack with the discharged-soldier credit (different statutes), subject to
  each cap.
- It applies against tax on income from personal effort? The statute says “בחישוב
  המס לשנת המס” without limiting to personal effort; the Tax Authority page speaks
  of reducing the tax on income. Use the annual computation, not a payroll-only
  assumption.
- The credit is claimed in the 101 for the current year based on last year’s
  service; if the employee did not claim it, the annual return can correct it.

## 4. Implementation

1. Ask for reserve days **in the previous tax year**, not the current year.
2. Validate the tier and cap; do not accept 0–29 days as a claim.
3. Require the IDF approval before allowing the claim.
4. Store the service year and the claim year separately.
5. For 2026, the maximum is 4 points = 11,616 ₪/year.
6. Do not apply the 2025 permanent rule to 2026 or vice versa.

## 5. Unverified

- Whether the credit is limited to income from personal effort in payroll (the
  statutory wording is broad; the Tax Authority page is not explicit).
- The treatment of reserve days in the year of discharge or in a year with no
  taxable income.
