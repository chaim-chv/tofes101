# 2026 brackets

## 1. The mid-year change you must not miss

The 2026 brackets in the original January booklet were the 2025 thresholds
(20% to 193,800; 31% to 269,280). On **31.3.2026** the Knesset passed the 2026
budget law, which amended s. 121 **retroactively from 1.1.2026**:

- 20% band extended to **228,000 ₪/year**;
- 31% band extended to **301,200 ₪/year**;
- 35% band unchanged at 560,280 ₪;
- 47% above.

The April 2026 update of the official booklet contains the corrected table.

## 2. Annual (personal effort / work income)

| Rate | Taxable annual income | Tax on the band |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10% | up to 84,120 ₪ | up to 8,412 ₪ |
| 14% | 84,121–120,720 ₪ | up to 5,124 ₪ |
| 20% | 120,721–**228,000** ₪ | up to 21,456 ₪ |
| 31% | 228,001–**301,200** ₪ | up to 22,692 ₪ |
| 35% | 301,201–560,280 ₪ | up to 90,678 ₪ |
| 47% | above 560,280 ₪ | — |

## 3. Monthly equivalent

| Rate | Monthly income |
| --- | --- |
| 10% | up to 7,010 ₪ |
| 14% | 7,011–10,060 ₪ |
| 20% | 10,061–**19,000** ₪ |
| 31% | 19,001–**25,100** ₪ |
| 35% | 25,101–46,690 ₪ |
| 47% | above 46,690 ₪ |

## 4. Daily table (for daily workers)

The 2026 booklet gives a daily table: 10% up to 280.40 ₪; 14% to 402.40; 20% to
760.00; 31% to 1,004.00; 35% to 1,867.60; 47% above. Use it only for the “עובד
יומי” salary type, and only when the daily table is the correct track.

## 5. Surtax

- **3%** on taxable income above **721,560 ₪/year** (60,130 ₪/month).
- **+2%** on capital-source income above the same threshold.
- The threshold was not indexed in 2025–2027 due to the freeze.

## 6. Worked example

Monthly salary 20,000 ₪, 2.25 points, no other income:

```
annual gross       = 240,000
tax:
  84,120 × 10%     =  8,412
  (120,720-84,120) × 14% = 5,124
  (228,000-120,720) × 20% = 21,456
  (240,000-228,000) × 31% = 3,720
  total tax        = 38,712
credits            = 2.25 × 2,904 = 6,534
tax after credits  = 32,178
monthly withhold   = 2,681.5
```

This is an estimate; real payroll handles deductions, benefits and non-fixed pay.

## 7. Notes

- The 2026 point value is 2,904 ₪/year; the freeze applies.
- The 2026 table is the one to use for a 2026 tax year, even for January–March
  2026 payroll that was originally calculated with the old thresholds. Employers
  were required to correct.
- See [[law/tax-brackets-2025]] for the 2025 table and [[law/credit-points-2026]]
  for the other 2026 numbers.
