# 2026 snapshot

Use this node when you need one page with the 2026 numbers. Every value is
year-stamped; do not reuse it for 2025.

## 1. Core values

| Item | 2026 value |
| --- | --- |
| Credit point | **2,904 ₪/year · 242 ₪/month** |
| Resident base | 2.25 points (2 + 0.25 travel) = 6,534 ₪/year |
| Female resident | 2.75 points = 7,986 ₪/year |
| Youth 16–17 | +1 point |
| Max withholding rate | 47% |
| Surtax | 3% above 721,560 ₪/year |
| Capital surtax | +2% above 721,560 ₪/year |
| Spouse-income threshold | 25,410 ₪/year |
| Disability/blind exemption ceilings | 684,000 / 445,200 / 81,960 ₪ |

## 2. Brackets (annual / monthly)

| Rate | Annual up to | Monthly up to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10% | 84,120 ₪ | 7,010 ₪ |
| 14% | 120,720 ₪ | 10,060 ₪ |
| 20% | **228,000 ₪** | **19,000 ₪** |
| 31% | **301,200 ₪** | **25,100 ₪** |
| 35% | 560,280 ₪ | 46,690 ₪ |
| 47% | above | above |

The 20%/31% thresholds were widened by the law of 31.3.2026, effective
**retroactively from 1.1.2026**. A payroll that used the 2025 thresholds
(193,800 / 269,280) must correct.

## 5. New 2026 temporary oleh exemption

The 2026 budget law added a **temporary income-tax exemption** for olim and
returning residents who arrive between **5.11.2025 and 31.12.2026**:

| Year | Exemption ceiling |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 | 600,000 ₪ |
| 2027 | 1,000,000 ₪ |
| 2028 | 1,000,000 ₪ |
| 2029 | 350,000 ₪ |
| 2030 | 150,000 ₪ |

This is **in addition to** the s. 35 credit points. It applies to income from
work and business produced in Israel. The detailed circular was still pending at
the time of the 2026 booklet; treat the operational details as `likely` until the
circular is published. See [[law/new-immigrant]].

## 6. What is frozen

- Point value 2024–2027.
- Most indexed amounts for 2025–2027 (freeze law).
- Surtax threshold 721,560 (not indexed in this period).
- Spouse-income threshold 25,410 (2025–2026).
- Disability/blind ceilings 684,000 / 445,200 / 81,960 (2025–2026).

## 7. What changed in 2026

1. 20% bracket widened from 193,800 to 228,000; 31% from 269,280 to 301,200
   (retroactive to 1.1.2026).
2. New temporary oleh/returning-resident exemption (arrivals from 5.11.2025).
3. Reserve-combatant credit temporary regime (2026–2027).
4. Electronic-101 rules for 2026 (circular 12.1.2026).
5. Settlement-benefit changes for 2026–2029 and new localities (not core to the
   101 form, but item 3 may be affected — check the current locality list).
