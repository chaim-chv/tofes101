# Disability / blindness exemption

## 1. What it is

Section 9(5) grants an **exemption from tax on income**, not credit points. Form
101 section ח item 2 is a request for that exemption.

| Situation | 2025–2026 ceiling |
| --- | --- |
| Blind or 100% disabled, **receiving** a monthly stipend under the Disabled Persons Law / Victims of Hostilities Law | **684,000 ₪/year** |
| Blind or 100% disabled, **not** receiving such a stipend, income from personal effort | **445,200 ₪/year** |
| Blind or 100% disabled, income **not** from personal effort | **81,960 ₪/year** |

The ceilings are frozen for 2025–2026 (freeze law); the 2026 booklet confirms the
same values as 2025.

## 2. Conditions

1. The employee is **blind** or **100% disabled** as defined in s. 9(5), or is a
   disabled/ blind person receiving a monthly stipend under the Disabled Persons
   Law or the Victims of Hostilities Law.
2. The employee has the required certificate:
   - approval from the Ministry of Defense / Treasury / assessor, or
   - a blind certificate issued **after 1.1.1994**.
3. For the higher 684,000 ceiling, the employee must also receive the monthly
   stipend (item 2ב); 2ב can only be ticked if 2א is ticked.
4. The exemption is applied to the relevant income; the employer may need an
   assessor approval/coordination because the payroll may not be able to apply the
   full annual ceiling monthly.

## 3. Form 101 item 2

- **2א**: “אני נכה 100% / עיוור/ת לצמיתות” + certificate.
- **2ב**: “בנוסף, אני מקבל תגמול חודשי לפי חוק הנכים (תגמולים ושיקום) או לפי חוק
  התגמולים לנפגעי פעולות איבה” + approval. Only if 2א.
- The form note (14) is explicit: 2ב only with 2א, and only that way gets the
  higher exemption ceiling.
- If the employee has other income and did not tick “no other income” in section
  ה, the form sends them to the assessor for coordination before applying the
  exemption.

## 4. What it is not

| Not this | Correct |
| --- | --- |
| 2.25 credit points | the 2.25 resident points are separate and still apply |
| A fixed 2 points | the exemption is an income ceiling, not a point |
| The disabled-child credit (s. 45, 2 points) | that is for a child; this is the employee’s own exemption |
| A blanket exemption for all income | the ceiling depends on the income type and the stipend |

## 5. Implementation

1. Do not add “2.25” or any points for item 2; model it as an exemption with a
   ceiling and an income-type selector.
2. Require the certificate and, for 2ב, the stipend approval.
3. If 2ב is ticked without 2א, reject.
4. If the employee has other income, show the assessor-coordination warning.
5. Display the ceiling year-stamped (684,000 / 445,200 / 81,960 for 2025–2026).
6. Do not compute the exemption in the payroll without the assessor’s instruction;
   the employer may only apply what the approval allows.

## 6. Unverified

- The exact monthly mechanics for applying an annual ceiling through payroll; the
  form/assessor approval is the practical guide.
- Whether the 2026 temporary changes affect these ceilings (the freeze law says no
  indexation; the values are unchanged).
