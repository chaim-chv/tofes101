# Declaration and signature

## 1. The declaration text (paraphrase)

The employee declares that:

1. the details in the form are **complete and correct**;
2. they know that **omission or false details** are an offence under the Income Tax
   Ordinance;
3. they undertake to **notify the employer of any change** in their personal
   details or the declared particulars **within 7 days of the change**.

The form has a date field and a signature field. The declaration is the legal
representation; it is **not** a privacy consent and does not waive any right.

## 2. Paper form

- The employee signs and dates section י.
- The employer fills section א (and may fill its own details), verifies the
  attachments, and keeps the form.
- There is **no employer signature field** on the paper form. The employer’s
  control is the payroll computation and the archive, not a signature on the
  employee’s declaration.
- A stamped/company stamp is not a legal substitute for the employee’s signature.

## 3. Approved electronic form (2026)

The 2026 circular requires:

1. employee identification by a **personal password** (unique);
2. display of the declaration and an **active confirmation** (checkbox) before
   proceeding;
3. re-entry of the password before the form is locked;
4. the employer’s **validation/approval** of the form and attachments;
5. locking as a **PDF signed with an approved electronic signature** under the
   Electronic Signature Law, 2001;
6. the signature date visible on the form;
7. notification to the employee of acceptance/rejection;
8. a versioned archive of all versions.

An unfinished process is a “טופס לקוי” and the employer must withhold at the
maximum rate.

## 4. What a browser-generated PDF is

- It is a **paper-form equivalent**: the employee can print, sign by hand and give
  it to the employer.
- A drawn/typed signature in the browser is **not** an approved electronic
  signature under the 2001 Law unless the implementation meets the law’s
  requirements (certificate, identity verification, etc.). Do not label it
  “חתימה אלקטרונית מאושרת”.
- It does not replace the employer’s approved electronic-101 system. If the
  employer uses one, the employee may need to enter the data there and sign in
  that system.
- Do not claim the PDF is “legally binding” without qualification; say “טופס לחתימה
  והגשה למעסיק”.

## 5. Who can sign

- The employee (or a person with a power of attorney / guardian appointment, in
  the case of incapacity). The form does not provide a guardian signature block;
  attach the legal appointment.
- A minor can sign their own 101; no parent signature requirement was found.
  **Unverified** for very young minors; the employer should follow its legal
  policy.
- A spouse cannot sign for the employee without a power of attorney.

## 6. Date and versioning

- The date must be the actual signature date; do not prefill a date the employee
  did not sign.
- If the form is corrected after signature, create a **new version** with a new
  date; keep the old version. Do not edit a signed PDF.
- The employer’s archive must allow retrieval of every version (2026 circular).

## 7. Common errors

1. Applying a credit before the declaration is signed.
2. Treating the declaration as consent to share data with advertisers.
3. Using the previous year’s signed form for the new year.
4. Editing a signed PDF instead of creating a new version.
5. Claiming a drawn signature is an approved electronic signature.
6. Forgetting the 7-day change undertaking in the help text.
