# Discharged soldier credit

## 1. The rule

For a **חייל משוחרר** (discharged soldier as defined in the Discharged Soldiers
Law), the tax on income from personal effort includes a fraction of a point for
each of the **36 months after the month of discharge**:

| Service length | Monthly fraction | Effective points per year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full service — men 23+ months, women 22+ months | **1/6 point/month** | **2 points/year** |
| Less than full service | **1/12 point/month** | **1 point/year** |

- Starts **after the month of discharge** (the discharge month itself is not
  counted).
- Runs for 36 consecutive months.
- Applies to income from personal effort (work/business), not to passive income.
- It is **in addition** to the 2.25 resident base and any other credits.

Statutory basis: s. 39א.

## 2. National service

The form item 14 covers “חייל/ת משוחרר/ת / שירתתי בשירות לאומי”. The statute’s
s. 39א definition is tied to the Discharged Soldiers Law; national-service
graduates are treated similarly by the Tax Authority (1 point/year for 36 months).
The exact statutory instrument for national service is a regulation/instruction,
not s. 39א itself; mark the national-service amount `likely` and verify with the
Tax Authority’s national-service page.

## 3. Documents and form

- Form 101 section ח item 14.
- Service start date and end date are mandatory if the box is ticked.
- Attach a copy of the **discharge certificate** or national-service completion
  certificate.
- The employer should verify the dates and the service length; the credit starts
  the month after discharge.

## 4. Interaction with other tracks

- A discharged soldier can also be a youth (16–17) or a student; the credits stack
  unless a specific rule says otherwise.
- A soldier who served less than 12 months may still qualify if discharged early
  for health/disability reasons (Kol Zchut); the statute’s 1/12 track covers
  partial service.
- Reserve combatant credit (s. 39ב) is separate and can stack with the discharged-
  soldier credit, subject to the reserve cap.

## 5. Implementation

1. Store discharge date and service length; compute the 36-month window.
2. Apply 1/6 or 1/12 per month, not a flat 1 or 2 points per year.
3. Do not apply the credit before the month after discharge.
4. Do not apply it to passive income.
5. Require the certificate before allowing the claim.
6. Year-stamp: the 36-month window can span tax years; compute per month.

## 6. Unverified

- The exact national-service statutory basis and whether it is exactly 1 point/year
  for all national-service tracks.
- The treatment of a soldier discharged mid-month (the statute says “after the
  month”, so the first full month after discharge).
