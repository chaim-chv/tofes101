# Who must file

## 1. The default rule

1. **Every employee** (עובד) who receives a salary from an employer must fill
   form 101 at the start of the employment and at the start of every tax year.
   “עובד” = an individual receiving “משכורת”; “מעסיק” = a person paying it
   (regulation 1; form notes (1)).
2. The duty is on the **employee**; the employer has a separate duty to **demand**
   the form on the same dates (regulation 2(c)).
3. A pensioner receiving a pension is treated as an employee of the pension payer
   for this purpose (“משכורת” includes קצבה), so the same form is used, with
   section ד box 6.

## 2. Statutory exclusions (regulation 2(a))

| Person | Form 101? | What applies instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **עובד יומי** (daily worker) | No — expressly excluded from the employee duty | Daily withholding table (תוספת ב׳); the employer still withholds |
| **עובד זר** (foreign worker) | No — expressly excluded | Withholding under תוספת ג׳ (foreign resident) or תוספת א׳/ב׳ (resident); credit points under the foreign-worker regulations |
| Employee with **no Israeli ID** | Form supports passport + visa, but the electronic version hides the Israeli-resident/child/credit sections | Passport + residence permit; assessor instructions for withholding |
| **Pensioner 70+** | Administrative exemption from the *annual* renewal if no change and no special reliefs | Tax Authority guidance, not a change to regulation 2 |

The exclusion of daily/foreign workers does **not** mean “no tax”: it means the
employer does not need the declaration to apply the special withholding table.
If such a worker wants credits, the practical route is a voluntary form or the
annual return.

## 3. Pensioners and 101ג

- A pensioner may use the general form 101 or the shortened **101ג**.
- Tax Authority guidance exempts several pensioner groups from the annual form:
  70+ with no change/no other income; 3 years after retirement age → every 5
  years; survivors’ pensions; 100% nursing-level; disability/loss-of-capacity
  pensions every 2 years. Conditions include no special reliefs (e.g. settlement
  benefit) and the payer filing form 126 and using mechanised payroll.
- The exemption is **administrative**; the app should still offer the form if the
  pensioner wants to declare a change or a new credit.

## 4. Minors

- A 16–17-year-old employee is within the form’s scope: the form has item 13 for
  the youth credit (s. 40ב) and the payroll needs the declaration to apply it.
- No legal requirement for a parent/guardian signature was found in the form or
  the regulations. Employment of children under 15 is separately restricted by
  the Youth Labour Law; that is outside the 101.
- If the minor cannot sign, the employer should follow its own legal-capacity
  policy and, where in doubt, the assessor’s instructions. **Unverified.**

## 5. Multiple employers

- The employee fills a **separate 101 for each employer** (each payroll needs its
  own declaration).
- Only one employer should apply the credit points/brackets; the others must be
  told “I have other income / credits are used elsewhere” (section ה item 8) and
  will withhold at the maximum rate unless coordination allocates otherwise.
- See [[law/tax-coordination]].

## 6. Employees who are also self-employed

- Income from a business is **not** reported in form 101. The form covers salary,
  pension and scholarships; business income is handled in the annual return
  (form 1301) or a tax-coordination request (form 116/online).
- Do not add a “business income” field to the 101; the correct action is a
  coordination request.

## 7. What the employer must do when the employee refuses

- Demand the form in writing (regulation 2(c)).
- If it is still missing, withhold at the maximum rate (regulation 5(a)) and
  document the demand. The employer cannot simply apply the previous year’s form:
  the declaration is annual.
- Escalate to payroll/legal; no explicit statutory fine on the employee for mere
  non-submission was found (see [[law/edge-cases]]).
