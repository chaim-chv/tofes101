**Privacy (the load-bearing parts):**
- §2 free service, funded by ads, commercial collaborations, market research, surveys,
  **cross-referencing with other databases**, marketing, commercial use of submitted
  data.
- §2.3 no transfer to third parties without a dedicated opt-in, "except where
  permitted/required by law"; in some cases the service may be **conditioned** on
  consent.
- §5 purposes include statistical analysis, segmentation, "creation of user groups with
  similar characteristics", business insights.
- §7.8 on consent, transfer to business partners, advertisers, research/survey firms:
  contact details, demographics, usage data, "general characteristics".
- §7.9 data transfers in a merger/acquisition, **including marketing/research
  databases**.
- §7.10 in-form commercial campaigns: details may go to the advertiser.

**Terms:** a generic SaaS agreement for the *paid* Tepez platform (sections "התשלום
עבור המערכת", "תקופת ניסיון", "ביטול השירות והחזרים כספיים"), last revised 2019. It
does not describe the free consumer service it is attached to.

**Accessibility:** claims AA per Israeli reg. 5568 + WCAG 2.0, lists three adjustments
(browser support, Ctrl+/-, keyboard), contact `accessibility@tepez.co.il`.
