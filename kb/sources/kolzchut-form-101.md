# Kol Zchut — form 101 procedure

Plain-Hebrew procedure: who must file, the 7-day/first-payslip deadline, the
1-January renewal, the 7-day update duty, what to attach (ID + ספח, certificates),
and the meaning of each credit/exemption item. Also records the pensioner
exemptions and the fact that credit points are for Israeli residents only.
Used as a cross-check, never as the sole basis for a numeric value.
