# Kol Zchut — alimony to a former spouse

One point for a divorced person who has remarried and who (or whose spouse) pays
alimony to the former spouse. Starts in the year of the remarriage and is granted
for the full year. This is **not** the child-support credit; child support is
handled through the single-parent/children rules. Statutory basis s. 40א.
