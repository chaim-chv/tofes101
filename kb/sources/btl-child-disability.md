# NII child-disability allowance + tax credit

NII leaflet: a parent of a child up to 18 receiving גמלת ילד נכה may claim
**2 credit points per year**, for one parent only (with alternatives for
separated parents). Confirms the form-101 item 11 condition: current-year NII
approval, child under 19, no income of the child, and the other parent not
claiming the same points.
