# Privacy Protection Law Amendment 13

Official professional guide published 14.8.2025, the day Amendment 13 entered
into force. It describes the expanded definition of personal data, the narrowed
database-registration duty, notification for large/sensitive databases, the DPO
duty, administrative fines and new criminal offences. For a local-only tool the
key question is whether the developer ever receives/stores personal data; if not,
the employer remains the controller and the developer is outside the database.
