# New-immigrant tax guide

For olim/returning residents from 1.1.2022: 54 months of credit — 1 point in
months 1–12, **3 points/month in months 13–30**, 2 points/month in months 31–42,
1 point/month in months 43–54. For olim before 2022: 42 months at 3 / 2 / 1.
Value 242 ₪/month per point (2026). The guide also links the 2026–2030 temporary
exemption for olim/returning residents arriving 5.11.2025–31.12.2026.
