# ת"י 5568 חלק 1

The official gov.il page "תקנים ישראליים בנושא נגישות" publishes the **full text
of IS 5568 parts 1 and 2** as PDF (all other standards were removed from the site).

- Part 1: `sitedocs_si-5568-1-september-2023.pdf` — "קווים מנחים לנגישות תכנים
  באינטרנט: נגישות אתרי אינטרנט".
- Part 2: `sitedocs_si-5568-2-september-2023.pdf` — accessibility of digital
  documents.

## What it establishes (read from the PDF foreword, 2026-10-29)

| Fact | Text in the standard |
| --- | --- |
| Edition | ספטמבר 2023 (תשרי התשפ"ד), replaces the May 2021 edition |
| Normative basis | "זהה למסמך … **WCAG 2.0 – 11 December 2008**, למעט השינויים והתוספות הלאומיים" |
| Scope | websites; part 2 covers digital documents |
| Legal force | becomes official 60 days after publication in Reshumot, unless stated otherwise |

**Consequence for us:** Israeli law today points at **WCAG 2.0 level AA**, not
2.2. WCAG 2.2 is a superset we adopt voluntarily for quality — see
[[ux/a11y-is5568-vs-wcag22]].

The regulation that makes this standard binding (level AA) is quoted in
[[source/israel-regs-service-accessibility]].
