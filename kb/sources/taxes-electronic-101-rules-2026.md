# Circular 000058/2026 — electronic 101 for tax year 2026

23 pages, dated 12.1.2026. Establishes:

- the regulation-2 duty and the 1-January renewal;
- the conditions for employers to replace the annual paper form with an approved
  electronic system (employee password, declaration, employer e-signature, PDF
  lock, versioned archive);
- **נספח א**: a field-by-field matrix of required/optional, allowed values and
  attachments for every section (א–י). This is the best machine-readable source
  for conditional logic (e.g. children rows, spouse block, items 7/8).
- a completed process is required; otherwise the form is “לקוי” and tax is
  withheld at the maximum rate.
