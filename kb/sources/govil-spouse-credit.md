# Dependent-spouse credit

One point for a Israeli resident who supports a spouse living with them, where the
spouse’s income does not exceed **25,410 ₪/year (2026)**, and one of the spouses
has reached retirement age or is blind/disabled under s. 9(5). The page lists the
disability thresholds (90%+; 40%+ specific impairment; 185+ days) and requires a
registered marriage/joint household.
