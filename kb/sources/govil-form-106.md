# Form 106 — annual employee statement

The employer must give every employee the completed form 106 **by 31 March** of
the year after the tax year. On termination, it is given at the end of the
employment. Form 106 is the annual reconciliation document built from the 101 and
the payroll; it is not part of the 101 itself.
