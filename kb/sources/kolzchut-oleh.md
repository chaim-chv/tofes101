# Kol Zchut — oleh credit points

Confirms 42 months for olim before 2022 and 54 months for 2022+, with the
1 / 3 / 2 / 1 monthly point schedule and the annual computation (1/12, 1/4, 1/6,
1/12 of a point per month). Notes that the credit applies to all taxable income,
not only work income, and can be claimed retroactively up to 6 years.
