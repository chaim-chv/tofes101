# Kol Zchut — discharged soldiers / national service

36 months of credit from the month after discharge: 2 points/year if full service
(23+ months for men, 22+ for women), 1 point/year for 12–22 months. National
service graduates get 1 point/year for 36 months. The statutory mechanism is
1/6 or 1/12 of a point per month (s. 39א).
