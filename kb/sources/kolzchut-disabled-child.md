# Kol Zchut — disabled child

Two points per year for parents of a “child without capacity” under s. 45, value
5,808 ₪/year (2026). Applies to a child (including an adult child) meeting the
statutory criteria; one parent (or both separated parents, if the child receives
NII disability allowance and they do not share a household). Alternative: the
institution-care credit.
