# Bookkeeping rules — retention

Section 25(ג): the accounting system must be kept **7 years from the end of the
tax year** or **6 years from the date the annual return was filed**, whichever is
later. Section 25(ד) sets a 3-year minimum for other documents the business
chooses/ is required to keep. This is the general retention basis for payroll
records including form 101; the form-specific electronic-101 rules additionally
require a versioned archive.
