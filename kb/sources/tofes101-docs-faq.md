# `GET /docs/online-form/faq/`

**Status:** 200. 15 accordion triggers; only **3** have bodies (the three "about the
service" ones). The 9 employee and 3 employer questions are empty shells — one of them
is literally "איך עושים XYZ?".

Employee questions (real friction signals, no answers): city/street missing from the
database; don't know the employer's email; "a file I attached didn't appear in the
PDF"; "I made a mistake, how do I fix it"; "I don't know how to fill the form"; "you're
missing towns/streets!"; "I'd rather fill it on paper"; "how do I send to several
employers"; "I sent it to the wrong address, now what".

Employer questions: how to add bank details; how to update employer details/email in a
template I already created; "how do I do XYZ?".

Service answers (verbatim gist): (a) "we are Tepez, a company that builds form software;
**we built this form as a marketing tool to show our system's capabilities**";
(b) funding = ads, commercial collaborations, and use of the data users provide;
(c) "do you sell my information to third parties?" → **"כן"** (yes, with your consent,
as detailed in the privacy policy).
