# 2025 withholding booklet

Official 2025 tables. Credit point 242 ₪/month = 2,904 ₪/year (p. 10). Brackets:
10% to 84,120; 14% to 120,720; 20% to 193,800; 31% to 269,280; 35% to 560,280;
47% above. Sur-tax 3% above 721,560; the 2025–2027 freeze applies. The 2025
disability/blind ceilings are the same 684,000 / 445,200 / 81,960.
