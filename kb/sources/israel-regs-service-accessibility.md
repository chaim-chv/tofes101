# Service Accessibility Regulations — consolidated text

The gov.il guide links this Wikisource page as the full text. It contains the
2013 regulations **with the 2017 (תשע״ח) amendment merged in**; amended headings
are marked `[תיקון: תשע״ח]`. Wikisource is a mirror, not the gazette — for a
legal opinion, check the Reshumot/Nevo version.

> Numbering note: the Wikisource conversion drops most clause numbers from
> headings. The numbers below were reconstructed from the section order and
> from the regulation's own cross-references (35ה cites 34 and 91(ה); 35ב
> cites 35ג for apps). Verify against Nevo/Reshumot before quoting a clause
> number in a legal document.

## Section ג (internet services) — what it establishes

- **35ב(א)** — a liable party must make internet services accessible per the
  Israeli internet accessibility standard (IS 5568), at **level AA**.
- **35ב(ג)(2)** — a **form for filling in data in order to receive a service**
  must comply, even if it was created before 26.10.2015. → Form 101 itself is
  squarely in scope.
- **35ב(ד)** — a deviation is not a violation unless the liable party was given
  written notice and did not fix it within a reasonable time, no later than
  **60 days**.
- **35ו(ז)** — exempt: עוסק פטור, or average annual turnover ≤ **₪100,000**.
- **35ו(ט)** — partial exemption for sites launched before the regulations with
  average turnover ≤ ₪1,000,000, valid 3 years at a time.
- **35ה** — accessibility statement: **in a prominent place** on the site,
  per the standard's guidelines, including (a) the accommodations made,
  (b) the accessibility coordinator's details if one must be appointed, and
  (c) contact details for reporting missing accessibility or requesting an
  accommodation, including a request under 35א(ד)(1).
- **35ו(ג)** — a liable party that received an exemption must publish it and the
  alternative arrangements in the statement.

## Section ז — accessibility coordinator

- **91(א)** — an employer of **25 or more employees** must appoint a coordinator
  from among the employees.
- **91(ה)** — must publish to the public and inform employees of the
  coordinator's **name, office location and contact channels** (publication per
  regulation 34).
- **91(ד)** — the role: give the public information about accommodations; advise
  and train the liable party.
