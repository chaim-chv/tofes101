# Kol Zchut — foreign-worker credit points

From 2018: legal foreign workers in **nursing care** get 2.25 points; in other
sectors 1 point; foreign women get an extra 0.5 point in every sector. Asylum
seekers with a 2A5 temporary permit get no points. A worker present only part of
the year gets points pro rata by months in Israel. Statutory basis:
תקנות מס הכנסה (נקודות זיכוי לעובד זר).
