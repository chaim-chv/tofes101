# 2026 withholding booklet

Official monthly/annual withholding tables and indexed amounts. Key values:

- credit point 242 ₪/month = 2,904 ₪/year (p. 12);
- brackets after the 31.3.2026 widening: 10% to 84,120; 14% to 120,720; 20% to
  228,000; 31% to 301,200; 35% to 560,280; 47% above (p. 10);
- sur-tax 3% above 721,560 ₪/year (p. 11), plus the 2% capital-income sur-tax;
- disability/blind exemption ceilings 684,000 / 445,200 / 81,960 (p. 13);
- the freeze law: 2025–2027 amounts are not indexed (p. 3).
