# Form 116 — tax relief / coordination application

Two-page form to the assessor (or representative) listing all income sources,
employers, deduction-file numbers, gross income and tax withheld, plus the
requested reliefs. Its explanatory note states that, with the employee’s and
employer’s agreement, coordination can be done by the employer on form 101
without going to the assessor. This is the correct form for coordination.
