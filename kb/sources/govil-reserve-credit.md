# Reserve combatants’ credit (temporary 2026–2027)

Under s. 39ב, for tax years 2026 and 2027, credit is based on reserve combat
days in the previous tax year: 30–39 days = 0.5 point; 40–49 = 0.75; 50+ = 1;
plus 0.25 per additional 5 days, capped at **4 points**. Requires IDF approval.
The page confirms the point value 2,904 ₪/year for 2026–2027.
