# Kol Zchut — child credit pages

Covers the three linked pages (under 6, ages 6–17, up to 18). Key table for
2024–2026: mother/custodial parent 2.5 / 4.5 / 3.5 / 2.5 / 2 / 0.5 by age band
(birth, 1–2, 3, 4–5, 6–17, 18); father/non-custodial parent 2.5 / 4.5 / 3.5 /
2.5 / 1 / —. Explains that the under-6 grant goes to the parent who does not claim
the “until 18” track, and that points are annual and not prorated by birth month.
Sub-pages: `.../להורה_לילד_עד_גיל_5`, `.../להורים_לילדים_בגיל_17-6`,
`.../להורה_לילד_עד_גיל_18`.
