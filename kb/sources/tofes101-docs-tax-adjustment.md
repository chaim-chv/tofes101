# `GET /docs/tax-adjustment/`

**Status:** 200. ~853 Hebrew words — the longest single explainer on the site.

**Structure:** intro (why withholding is an estimate) → "when is coordination worth it"
(2 cases: multiple income sources; a gap in income during the year) → worked numeric
examples (10,000 ₪ × 12 vs × 10) → "how to do it", four routes with pros/cons:
1. through the employer, inside form 101 section ט (simplest, only simple cases; the
   employer may refuse; only the *primary* employer can apply credit points);
2. online, the Tax Authority's own questionnaire (limits: already coordinated this
   year, annual income above 444,480 ₪, credit types not in the system);
3. at the assessor (טופס 116, paper or computerised, attach documents, submit by post /
   in person);
4. through a representative (רו"ח / tax advisor, טופס 117, paid, not available above a
   threshold).

**Notes:** figures are stamped to 2016 in the body ("נכון לשנת 2016", 48 % max rate,
444,480 ₪ cap) — stale content published as current. Do not inherit those numbers.
