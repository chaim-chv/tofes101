# gov.il guide: website accessibility

Official explanatory guide to the Service Accessibility Regulations, section ג
(internet services). It links to the consolidated regulation text.

## What it establishes

- A service to the public provided **through the internet** must be accessible;
  this includes sites and apps that provide the service *or information about it*.
- The operative provisions are **סימן ג׳ לפרק ה׳** of
  תקנות שוויון זכויות לאנשים עם מוגבלות (התאמות נגישות לשירות), התשע״ג-2013.
- The 2017 amendment (published 26.10.2017) added the website-specific duties:
  level AA, digital documents, accessibility statement, exemptions.
- Exemptions exist, including a small-turnover exemption (see the regulation
  text in [[source/israel-regs-service-accessibility]]).

The guide is the authoritative *plain-language* explanation; the regulation text
is authoritative for the duties themselves. Use both, never only the guide.
