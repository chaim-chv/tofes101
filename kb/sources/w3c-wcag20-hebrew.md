# WCAG 2.0 בעברית

The Hebrew translation of WCAG 2.0 hosted by ISOC-IL; gov.il links to it from its
own accessibility statement. This is the version Israeli law references through
IS 5568 ([[source/govil-is5568-standard]]).

Use it for: quoting criteria to Hebrew-speaking stakeholders, and checking that
our interpretation matches the Hebrew wording the regulator expects. For
implementation details, use the English normative text and Understanding docs.
