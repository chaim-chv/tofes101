# NN/g top-10 form recommendations

Research-based heuristics, used here as secondary evidence:

1. Keep forms short; every field costs completion.
2. One column; labels above fields (works in RTL too).
3. Mark required vs optional clearly and consistently.
4. Don't use placeholder text as a label (it disappears; low contrast).
5. Group related fields; use fieldsets/legends.
6. Validate inline after the user leaves a field, not on every keystroke.
7. Show errors next to the field, in plain language, with a fix.
8. Preserve entered data on error — never clear the form.
9. Use `autocomplete` to reduce typing.
10. Make the primary action obvious; secondary actions visually quieter.

The article is older than WCAG 2.2; where it conflicts with 3.3.7 (redundant
entry) or 3.3.8, WCAG wins.
