# Payroll withholding regulations

Consolidated text of the 1993 regulations. Regulation 2 is the legal source of
form 101: who must fill it, the 7-day/1-January deadlines, the employer’s duty to
demand it, the 7-day change notice, and the regulation-2(e) exemption for
approved mechanised employers. Regulation 3 sets monthly withholding; regulation
5(a) imposes maximum withholding when the form (or its other-income section) is
missing; regulation 6 covers special cases (including payments to heirs).
