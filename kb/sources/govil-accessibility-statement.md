# A real, compliant-looking accessibility statement

The National Digital Agency's own statement. Useful as a content template, not as
a legal template.

## Structure it demonstrates

1. **Why** — ~20% of the population needs digital accessibility.
2. **Conformance level** — the Service Accessibility Regulations 2013, **AA**,
   plus WCAG 2.0.
3. **What was done** — navigation, headings, alt text, resize, no flashing text,
   plain language.
4. **Known gaps / exemptions** — explicitly lists temporarily exempt systems and
   the date/authority of the exemption.
5. **Coordinator** — link to the list of ministry coordinators.
6. **How to report a problem** — email + the exact details to include
   (description, action attempted, page URL, browser+version, OS, AT used).
7. **Date / review cadence** — implied by "updated" metadata.

**Note:** the statement claims WCAG 2.0 + AA, consistent with IS 5568. Do not
copy "we meet WCAG 2.2" into our statement unless we actually test 2.2.
