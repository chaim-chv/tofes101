# `GET /docs/online-form/custom-form/`

**Status:** 200.

**What it establishes:**
- Definition of a "custom org form": employer details are pre-filled in the employer
  section and **the employee cannot change them**; when the employee submits, the form
  arrives at the employer's email. Stated main benefit: "prevents mistakes typing the
  email address".
- Build steps: open the builder → fill org details + choose the receiving email →
  press "צור את הטופס" → the link shown on screen is your form; copy and keep it.
- WhatsApp caveat: a link is only tappable for the employee if the sender's number is
  in the employee's contacts; otherwise it shows as plain text and must be pasted into
  a browser.
- Live example link: `https://tpz.link/81dzq`.
