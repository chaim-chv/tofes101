# Online tax coordination

The Tax Authority’s online coordination service: for employees with more than one
employer/payer, pensioners, and some other income. The employee enters each
employer’s deduction-file number, expected annual income and tax already
withheld; the system issues certificates to give the employers. Coordination is
for the current tax year only; a draft can be resumed; a representative can be
authorised. It is the online counterpart of form 116, not form 119.
