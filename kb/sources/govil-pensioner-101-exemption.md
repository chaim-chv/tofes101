# Pensioner exemptions from annual 101

Tax Authority guidance: pensioners aged 70+ who declare no additional taxable
income and no special reliefs (e.g. settlement benefit) are exempt from the annual
101. Other tracks: 3 years after retirement age → form every 5 years; survivors’
pensions; 100% nursing-level pensions; disability/loss-of-capacity pensions every
2 years. The exemption is an administrative instruction, not a change to
regulation 2.
